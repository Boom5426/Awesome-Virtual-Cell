## Follow-up: Intervention Design expansion

Added six source-checked records to strengthen the inverse/intervention-design track:

- **VCDesign** — finite-budget candidate ranking toward a desired cellular target state; the public `Boom5426/VCDesign-CED` repository is the paper and implementation source.
- **PDGrapher** — causally inspired therapeutic target/combinatorial perturbation prediction for moving diseased transcriptional states toward treated states.
- **PAIRING** — explicitly identifies perturbations that induce a desired cell state; Cell Systems links its Zenodo code archive.
- **PerturbNet** — learns perturbation-to-state distributions and includes design of perturbations toward a desired cell-state distribution.
- **ARC** — reverse-control target identification to restore nominal phenotype landscapes.
- **NUDGE** — minimal temporary interventions for desired cell-fate control while preserving natural dynamics.

**CellNavi** was already present and remains a core `Perturbation` + `Intervention Design` + `Gene Regulation` entry.

Primary sources include DOI `10.1038/s41551-025-01481-x` (PDGrapher), `10.1016/j.cels.2025.101405` (PAIRING), `10.1038/s44320-025-00131-3` (PerturbNet), `10.1126/sciadv.adw3995` (ARC), `10.1073/pnas.2604777123` (NUDGE), and the public `Boom5426/VCDesign-CED` repository.
